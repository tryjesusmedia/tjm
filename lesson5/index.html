The confidence game

The Most Effective Lies Look Official

Paris, 1925. Victor Lustig entered a luxury hotel carrying polished credentials, government stationery, and a secret opportunity.

01
The setting

A private suite, selected businessmen, and the atmosphere of an invitation few people would ever receive.

02
The claim

The French government had supposedly decided to dismantle the Eiffel Tower and sell it for scrap.

03
The leverage

Official-looking documents, secrecy, and quiet confidence made the impossible feel plausible.

04
The result

One businessman paid. The Eiffel Tower was never for sale. Lustig disappeared with the money.

People rarely surrender their trust to an obvious lie. They surrender it to a lie wrapped in authority.

The Eiffel Tower survived the scam. But another deception has distorted how millions of people see God.

The diagnostic

The Law Is Not the Cure.
It Is the Scan.

Imagine feeling that something is wrong physically but refusing every medical test. Without an accurate diagnosis, even the finest treatment becomes guesswork.

01 The symptoms

Conflict, addiction, dishonesty, shame, fractured trust, and the quiet sense that something within us is not whole.

02 The scan

God’s law brings hidden motives into view and gives a name to what is damaging love, character, and life.

03 The limit

A scan can reveal a fracture. It cannot perform the surgery. The law diagnoses; it does not regenerate.

THE KEY DISTINCTIONThe law reveals the fracture. It was never designed to become the remedy.

The distortion exposed

The Lie Was Not That God Has a Law

The lie was that His law is arbitrary.

THE DISTORTED PICTURE

Sin is harmless. God is dangerous.

God invents a rule. The rule has no natural connection to life. Someone breaks it. God becomes angry and imposes pain.

THE BIBLICAL PICTURE

Sin destroys. God rescues.

God’s commands describe how love, loyalty, truth, freedom, and human flourishing actually work. His judgment is the final end of evil—not petty retaliation.

God is not trying to protect His authority from you. He is trying to protect you from what destroys you.

This does not eliminate divine judgment. It changes how we understand it. Scripture presents God as patient, grieved by evil, committed to rescue, and determined to bring deception, exploitation, violence, and death to an end.

What about the Flood, Sodom, and Acts 5?+

These accounts deserve more than a quick slogan. The central question is not whether God ever judges, but whether His judgments are consistent with His character of love, justice, freedom, and mercy.

The historical record

A Law Written in Stone Met Laws Written by Men

The historical development of Sunday observance is more complex than a single meeting or decree. But the record raises a question every serious student of Scripture must confront.

Before 321
Sunday gatherings already existed

Some Christians gathered on the first day in remembrance of the resurrection before Constantine’s civil legislation.

A.D. 321
Constantine gave Sunday civil protection

His law restricted courts and much urban labor on the “venerable day of the sun,” while allowing agricultural exceptions.

4th century
Laodicea opposed Sabbath rest

Canon 29 instructed Christians not to rest on the Sabbath and encouraged honor of the Lord’s Day instead.

Today
The Catholic Catechism explains Sunday theology

It openly distinguishes Sunday from the seventh-day Sabbath and grounds Sunday observance in Christ’s resurrection and church tradition.

THE QUESTION BENEATH THE HISTORYWhen Scripture and later religious tradition appear to point in different directions, which one carries final authority?

Follow the evidence

The Sabbath Did Not Begin at Sinai

Before Israel existed, before Moses received the commandments, and before any denomination had formed, God rested, blessed the seventh day, and set it apart.

01
Creation

God rests, blesses, and sanctifies the seventh day.

02
The moral law

03
A sign of sanctification

God identifies the Sabbath as a sign that He is the One who makes His people holy.

04
Jesus and the apostles

Jesus observes the Sabbath, calls it a gift for humanity, and Acts records apostolic teaching on it.

05
The final generation

Revelation describes God’s faithful people as keeping His commandments and the faith of Jesus.

The gift hidden inside the command

The Sabbath Is Not Another Demand on Your Schedule

It is freedom from being owned by it.

For six days, life measures you by production, achievement, responsiveness, and performance. The Sabbath interrupts that system.

Once each week, God invites you to stop proving your worth—to step away from transactions, become fully present, and remember that your identity does not come from what you accomplish, earn, manage, or control.

WHAT YOU HAVE DISCOVERED
  • God’s law reveals His character.
  • Sin damages the people who practice it.
  • The law diagnoses what is wrong.
  • Jesus is the remedy.
  • Obedience is the fruit of salvation—not its price.
  • The Sabbath declares that God, not performance, has the final claim on your life.

A simple way to experience your first Sabbath+

Prepare before Friday evening so the day does not feel rushed. From Friday sunset to Saturday sunset:

  • Step away from ordinary business and unnecessary work.
  • Spend unhurried time in Scripture and prayer.
  • Worship with other believers.
  • Share meaningful time with family or trusted friends.
  • Enjoy nature, beauty, rest, hospitality, and acts of mercy.
  • Resist turning the day into another performance checklist.

The goal is not flawless performance. The goal is deeper trust and relationship with God.
