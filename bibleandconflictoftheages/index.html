The Bible and Conflict of the Ages

Read more about this journey

Scripture always comes first. The Bible is the foundation, the authority, and the heart of every assignment. The Conflict of the Ages commentary never replaces the Word of God—it simply accompanies Scripture, adds historical and spiritual context, and helps you pause over truths you might otherwise pass too quickly.

Entire Bible 5 companion books 1 unfolding story

This journey will take you through the entire Bible alongside the complete five-volume Conflict of the Ages set: Patriarchs and Prophets, Prophets and Kings, The Desire of Ages, The Acts of the Apostles, and The Great Controversy.

Imagine reaching the final reading having followed the great story from Creation to restoration—not as disconnected passages, but as one unfolding revelation of God’s love. Commit to the journey, take it at your own pace, and we believe you will be richly blessed by the time you spend in God’s Word. The blessing is not merely in finishing a plan; it is in meeting God in Scripture, reading after reading.
