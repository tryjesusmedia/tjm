Why the Sabbath becomes a sign
The Fourth Commandment Answers the Question of Ultimate Authority
Scripture repeatedly calls the Sabbath a sign between God and His people. Its meaning reaches far beyond attending a service on a particular day.

CREATIONYou are not self-madeThe seventh day identifies the Lord as Creator. Your worth does not depend upon productivity, visibility, or financial success.
REDEMPTIONFreedom is God’s giftDeuteronomy connects Sabbath with liberation from slavery and extends rest to the vulnerable.
SANCTIFICATIONGod changes youThe Sabbath is called a sign that the Lord—not human performance—sanctifies His people.

THE LARGER CONFESSIONGod is Creator. God is Redeemer. God is Sanctifier. Your life is received before it is achieved.

Adventist teachers also observe that the fourth commandment identifies God’s name, authority as Creator, and domain over heaven, earth, and sea. That resembles the identifying function of an official seal, although Exodus 20 does not itself define a three-part seal formula.

Sabbath rest is not a résumé presented to God. It is the confession that your life depends upon Him.

How Sunday became dominant
History Is More Complicated Than One Emperor and One Decree
Christians gathered on Sunday before Constantine. His AD 321 legislation did not invent Sunday worship—but it gave the day significant civil protection and political weight.

2ND CENTURYSunday gatherings already existedJustin Martyr described Christians assembling on the day associated with the resurrection.
AD 321Constantine gave Sunday civil recognitionCourts, urban populations, and workshops were ordered to rest on the “venerable day of the sun,” with agricultural exceptions.
4TH CENTURYLaodicea opposed Sabbath restingCanon 29 instructed Christians not to rest in the Jewish manner on Saturday and to honor the Lord’s Day when possible.
TODAYTwo authority claims remainCatholic teaching presents Sunday as fulfilling the Sabbath. Adventists argue Scripture never transfers the holiness of the seventh day.

CATHOLIC POSITION
Sunday is the Lord’s Day
Its observance is grounded in Christ’s resurrection and apostolic tradition and fulfills the Sabbath’s moral meaning.

ADVENTIST POSITION
The seventh day remains holy
The resurrection gives Sunday importance, but no biblical command transfers the Sabbath from the seventh day.

The disagreement is not whether Jesus rose on Sunday. It is whether later authority may replace the day identified in the commandment.

What the history does not prove+

Constantine did not invent all Christian Sunday worship.
Christians did gather on Sunday before AD 321.
Not every Sunday worshiper knowingly rejects Scripture.
The transition cannot be reduced to one simple pagan conspiracy.
Not every quotation circulated in Sabbath debates is authentic.

The historical development involved resurrection commemoration, Jewish-Christian separation, regional practices, imperial law, councils, and gradual theological normalization.
