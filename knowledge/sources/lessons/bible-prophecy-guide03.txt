The scam that looked official
A Dangerous Lie Rarely Looks Ridiculous
Victor Lustig persuaded successful business leaders that the French government had quietly decided to sell the Eiffel Tower for scrap.

THE SETTINGLuxury and credibilityA meeting at the Hôtel de Crillon gave the scheme an atmosphere of privilege, secrecy, and authority.
THE PROOFOfficial-looking documentsLetters, seals, specifications, and confident language made a fabricated sale feel legitimate.
THE HOOKExclusive knowledgeThe opportunity felt valuable partly because the public supposedly knew nothing about it.
THE RESULTA believable distortionThe tower was real. The maintenance concerns were plausible. The sale was a complete invention.

The most destructive spiritual deception does not always deny God. It presents a version of Him that people cannot trust.

THE DEEPER SCAM
What happens when God’s law is presented as proof that He is controlling rather than loving?

The law is the diagnosis
What You Cannot Identify, You Cannot Intentionally Treat
A skilled physician may deliver uncomfortable news. But the diagnosis is not the disease—it is the first step toward healing.

01The symptomsBroken trust, resentment, compulsive desire, fractured commitments, and inner unrest.
02The diagnostic toolGod’s commands reveal the choices and motives quietly damaging the life beneath the surface.
03The purposeThe law does not expose sin to humiliate you. It reveals what must be healed.

Paul wrote, “I would not have known sin except through the law.” Jesus then carried the diagnosis deeper: murder begins with contempt, adultery begins with cultivated desire, and religious performance can conceal pride.

THE DIAGNOSISGod’s law reveals what your choices are forming within you—not merely what other people can see.

Does criticism equal murder?+

Not every disagreement, correction, or criticism violates the commandment against murder. Jesus does teach that contempt, hatred, and dehumanizing speech belong to the same moral trajectory.
The point is not that every harsh word is identical to homicide. It is that God cares about the inward hostility from which outward harm grows.

The two-part deception
The Scam Distorts Both Law and Grace
Two opposite-sounding messages can keep a person equally distant from Jesus.

DECEPTION ONE
Perform well enough and God will accept you.
The law becomes a ladder. Religion becomes image management. The outcome is pride through comparison—or despair through failure.

DECEPTION TWO
Because Jesus saves, God’s law no longer matters.
Grace becomes permission to remain unchanged while sin continues damaging character, freedom, and relationships.

01 You are not saved by obedience.
02 You are saved from the power and consequence of sin.
03 Obedience becomes the fruit of a relationship already restored.

Grace forgives you, reconnects you to Christ, and begins producing a new life. It does not lower the value of love.

Are moral laws identical to physical laws?+

No. Gravity describes a regular physical relationship. Moral commandments address personal beings who can choose, love, repent, forgive, and accept responsibility.
The comparison is useful only in showing that reality has structure and choices have consequences. Moral life must never be reduced to a mechanical formula.

Consequences and judgment
Does Sin Harm Us—or Does God?
The honest biblical answer is more nuanced than either slogan.

NATURAL CONSEQUENCESSin carries destruction within itself.Deception produces distrust.Addiction reshapes desire.Pride resists correction.Repeated choices form character.

DIVINE JUDGMENTLove does not permit evil to rule forever.God defends victims.He restrains entrenched violence.He exposes hidden injustice.He brings rebellion to an end.

Scripture presents sin growing toward death. It also presents God as an active judge. The Flood, Sodom and Gomorrah, Ananias and Sapphira, and the final judgment cannot responsibly be reduced to impersonal cause and effect.

God is not arbitrary, sadistic, or eager to destroy. Yet love does not require Him to preserve violence and rebellion forever.

The Sabbath as a case study
The Commandment Christianity Reinterpreted
The fourth commandment identifies the seventh day as the Sabbath. Its history raises a serious question about Scripture, tradition, and authority.

CREATIONGod rested, blessed, and sanctified the seventh day.The Sabbath enters the biblical story before Israel and before human religious institutions.
LIBERATIONRest declares that people are more than their production.Deuteronomy connects Sabbath with release from slavery and domination.
JESUSChrist observed the Sabbath and called Himself its Lord.He opposed legalistic distortions while preserving the day as a gift.
HISTORYSunday observance developed gradually.Early Christians gathered on Sunday; Constantine later gave it civil status, and church councils reinforced it.

A FAIR DISTINCTIONThis guide does not claim Constantine invented Sunday worship, that Sunday-keeping Christians are insincere, or that Saturday observance earns salvation. It presents the Adventist conclusion that Scripture never transfers the Sabbath’s sanctity from the seventh day to the first.

Inspect the historical record+

Sunday gatherings existed before Constantine. His A.D. 321 law later restricted many urban activities on the “venerable day of the Sun.” Canon 29 of the Council of Laodicea then instructed Christians not to rest on Saturday and to honor the Lord’s Day instead.

The question is not whether sincere Christians worship on Sunday. It is whether ecclesiastical tradition possesses authority to replace the specific seventh day identified in the commandment.

The remedy behind the law
Jesus Is Not the Alternative to God’s Character
The gospel is not a story in which Jesus rescues humanity from an unloving Father. God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself.

REVELATIONJesus makes the Father visible.His compassion, truth, courage, and self-sacrifice reveal what God has always been like.
FORGIVENESSGrace removes condemnation.You are not invited to earn acceptance through anxious religious performance.
TRANSFORMATIONThe law moves from stone into the heart.The Holy Spirit begins forming the love the commandments described.

WHAT YOU HAVE DISCOVERED

God’s law reveals sin but cannot save from sin.
The commandments describe love in action.
Obedience cannot purchase salvation.
Grace does not make transformation unnecessary.
Judgment is real, but God is not arbitrary or sadistic.
Jesus reveals the Father rather than protecting us from Him.
The Sabbath presents a test case of Scripture, tradition, rest, and authority.

The deepest decision is not merely which day appears on your calendar. It is whether fear, convenience, tradition, or Jesus will have the final authority in shaping your faith.

What this guide is not saying+

That commandment keeping earns eternal life.
That every consequence is a direct punishment from God.
That Christians who disagree about Sabbath are dishonest.
That external Sabbath observance proves inward faithfulness.
That serious mental illness, addiction, or relational trauma can be cured by religious effort alone.
That God’s law should be enforced through civil coercion.

Truth is not another weapon with which to condemn people. Its purpose is freedom, healing, and a clearer relationship with Jesus.
