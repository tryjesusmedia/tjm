Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Try Jesus Media respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you request resources or register for a Bible discussion, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, and mobile number. Basic technical information may also be processed by our website and service providers to operate and secure the site.

How we use your information

We may use your information to deliver the requested Bible guides, send access links and reminders, invite you to optional online discussions, provide related encouragement, respond to questions, and improve our communications.

Email and text messages

By submitting a registration form, you agree to receive the resources you requested and occasional related email or text communications. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. You can unsubscribe from email using the link included in a message or reply STOP to text messages.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted service providers, such as email, text-messaging, website-hosting, and video-conferencing platforms, to deliver the services you requested. These providers process information on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices.

Data retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal obligations, and ministry administration. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable safeguards are used to protect information.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe at any time. You may also ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with your registration by replying to a Try Jesus Media email.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, reply to any message you receive from Try Jesus Media or contact the ministry through its published communication channels.
