Privacy matters here.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Try Jesus Media respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you request Bible guides or other resources, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, and mobile phone number.

How we use your information

We use your information to deliver requested resources, send relevant email or text updates, provide optional invitations to Bible discussions, improve our resources, and respond to questions.

Email and text messages

By submitting a form with the applicable consent language, you agree to receive the requested resources and occasional email or text communications from Try Jesus Media. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to a text message to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. You may unsubscribe from email using the link provided in each message.

How we protect information

We use reputable service providers and reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us deliver emails, text messages, forms, hosting, analytics, or other necessary services. These providers are permitted to use information only to perform services on our behalf.

Cookies and analytics

Our website and service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to understand website performance, remember preferences, and support form functionality.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. You may also unsubscribe from communications at any time.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general adult audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, reply to an email you received from Try Jesus Media or use the contact information listed in that communication.
