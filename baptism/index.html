Maybe you’ve believed in Jesus for years but never been baptized. Maybe you’ve drifted away. Or maybe you’re just beginning to wonder whether God has something more for your life.

Between work, family, and everyday responsibilities, it’s easy to keep putting that decision off.

But you don’t have to have everything figured out before you reach out.

Your next step can begin with a simple conversation.

What does God invite you to do?

“Each of you must repent of your sins and turn to God.”

Acts 2:38 · NLT excerpt

Repentance means turning away from sin and turning toward God. In that same passage, Peter invites people to be baptized in Jesus’ name for the forgiveness of their sins.

Baptism is a beautiful expression of faith and surrender: leaving your old life behind and beginning a new life with Christ.

Imagine living
at peace with God

“We have peace with God.”

Romans 5:1 · NLT excerpt

That peace comes through faith in Jesus and what He has done for us.

Following Him doesn’t mean every problem disappears. It means you can face tomorrow with hope, experience the joy of His forgiveness, and find security in His love.

You don’t have to let yesterday keep deciding your tomorrow.
