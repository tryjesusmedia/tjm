Two Journeys. One Life-Changing Story.

Choose the path that speaks most directly to the questions you are exploring. Each journey has its own guide numbers, progress, and connected sequence. One essential study appears in both journeys because it forms a bridge between knowing Jesus and understanding prophecy.

01 Relationship Journey Get to Know Jesus Discover what God is truly like, why humanity needs healing, and how Jesus restores our relationship with Him.

10 carefully connected guides

Move beyond religion and discover the Person at its center.

This journey explores suffering, sin, salvation, God’s character, Christ’s ministry, death, judgment, eternity, and the transformation that begins when worship becomes a way of life.

01 If God Is Good, Why Is There So Much Evil? Why would a loving, all-powerful God allow suffering? Explore the origin of evil, the meaning of human freedom, and what the cross reveals about God’s response to pain. → 02 The Problem Success Cannot Solve Beneath conflict, addiction, injustice, shame, and broken relationships lies a deeper spiritual problem. Discover how humanity became separated from God—and the solution Jesus offers every person. → 03 The Greatest Scam in History: The Lie That Distorted God’s Character God’s law has been misunderstood and turned into a system of fear, control, and human effort. Discover how His law actually reveals His love, character, and design for freedom—and why understanding it changes how we see God. → 04 The Sanctuary: God’s Blueprint of Salvation The ancient sanctuary was more than a tent or system of ceremonies. Every sacrifice, priest, furnishing, and service revealed another part of God’s plan to save humanity through Jesus. → 05 The Sanctuary Revealed: What Jesus Is Doing Now The sanctuary story did not end when the earthly temple disappeared. Discover how its symbols point to Christ’s continuing ministry and what His work means for believers today. → 06 The Day of Atonement: When Mercy Meets Judgment Why was one day considered the most solemn day of Israel’s year? Explore how the Day of Atonement reveals cleansing, accountability, forgiveness, judgment, and the ministry of Jesus. → 07 What Really Happens When You Die? Do people immediately enter heaven or hell? Can the dead see or communicate with the living? Set tradition aside and examine what the Bible consistently teaches about death and resurrection. → 08 Is Hell Eternal? The Truth About the Consuming Fire Does a loving God torment people forever, or has hell been misunderstood? Explore the Bible’s language of fire, destruction, justice, and the ultimate end of evil. → 09 The 1,000 Years: What Happens After Jesus Returns? Where are the saved, the lost, and Satan during the Millennium? Follow the biblical sequence of events and discover what happens before God creates a world without suffering. → 10 Wax On, Wax Off: The Secret of True Worship What can The Karate Kid teach us about worship? Discover how repeated choices shape character—and why worship is expressed through far more than songs, sermons, or time spent in church. →

02 Prophecy Journey Understand Bible Prophecy Follow the connected evidence in Daniel and Revelation and discover what these ancient prophecies reveal about our time.

9 connected prophecy guides

Replace speculation with a framework you can investigate.

This journey begins by testing the Bible’s prophetic credibility, then examines the Messiah, the antichrist, the final conflict over worship, America’s possible prophetic role, church history, God’s faithful people, and the biblical tests of a genuine prophet.

01 Can the Bible Really Predict the Future? Put the Bible’s credibility to the test through Daniel 2—an ancient prophecy that traces the rise and fall of world empires and points toward the kingdom God has promised to establish. → 02 The Prophecy That Pinpointed the Messiah Centuries before Jesus was born, the Bible revealed when the Messiah would appear. Follow the remarkable timeline of Daniel 9 and examine the evidence that points directly to Christ. → 03 The Greatest Scam in History: The Lie That Distorted God’s Character God’s law has been misunderstood and turned into a system of fear, control, and human effort. Discover how His law actually reveals His love, character, and design for freedom—and why understanding it changes how we see God. → 04 Who Is the Antichrist? Follow the Biblical Clues Move beyond sensational headlines, political theories, and speculation. Examine the identifying characteristics found in Daniel and Revelation and allow the Bible to interpret its own symbols. → 05 The Seal of God vs. the Mark of the Beast The final conflict is about far more than technology, economics, or an outward symbol. Discover what the Bible reveals about worship, authority, allegiance, and the choice every person must make. → 06 Does the Bible Predict the Rise of America? Revelation describes a powerful nation emerging during a pivotal moment in history. Examine the prophetic clues, America’s possible role in last-day events, and why religious liberty matters. → 07 One Bible. Why So Many Churches? Jesus prayed for unity, yet Christianity became divided into countless traditions and denominations. Trace how that division developed and discover how to evaluate beliefs through Scripture. → 08 How Can You Recognize God’s Church Today? Rather than beginning with a denomination or religious label, begin with the Bible. Explore the characteristics Scripture gives for identifying God’s faithful people in the last days. → 09 Does the Bible Predict a Last-Day Prophet? Throughout Scripture, God repeatedly sent a messenger before major moments of judgment, deliverance, and spiritual renewal. Examine that pattern and learn the biblical tests of a genuine prophet. →
