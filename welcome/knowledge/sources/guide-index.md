6. **The Day of Atonement: When Mercy Meets Judgment** — `/get-to-know-jesus/guide6/` — cleansing, accountability, forgiveness, and judgment through Jesus.
7. **What Really Happens When You Die?** — `/get-to-know-jesus/guide7/` — death, unconscious sleep, resurrection.
8. **Is Hell Eternal? The Truth About the Consuming Fire** — `/get-to-know-jesus/guide8/` — biblical fire, justice, final destruction, end of evil.
9. **The 1,000 Years: What Happens After Jesus Returns?** — `/get-to-know-jesus/guide9/` — Millennium sequence and final resolution of sin.
10. **Wax On, Wax Off: The Secret of True Worship** — `/get-to-know-jesus/guide10/` — worship, repeated choices, and character transformation.
