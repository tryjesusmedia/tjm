# KJV corpus source

The normalized 66-book KJV text in `kjv-66-books.txt` was derived from Project Gutenberg eBook #7999, **The Bible, King James Version, Complete Contents**.

Source used during build:
`https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/7999/pg7999.txt`

Normalization performed for retrieval quality:
- removed Project Gutenberg header/footer material,
- preserved all 31,094 verses in the source,
- converted numeric references such as `01:001:001` to standard references such as `Genesis 1:1`,
- joined line-wrapped verse text onto one verse line,
- retained the 66-book Protestant canon used by Pastor Kal's supplied teaching framework.

Project Gutenberg identifies its KJV material as public domain in the United States and advises users outside the United States to check applicable local law.
