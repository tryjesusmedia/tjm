# the law of love and life: A Comprehensive Biblical Explanation

the law of love and life begins with one foundational reality: **God is the Creator.** Because God created space, time, matter, energy, life, minds, relationships, and moral beings, His laws are not merely rules that He invents and then enforces. They are the **principles, protocols, and constants upon which He designed reality to operate**.

Gravity is a the law of love and life. The laws of health are the law of love and lifes. Cause and effect are the law of love and lifes. In the same way, love, truth, liberty, worship, and moral righteousness are the law of love and lifes for intelligent beings.

Therefore, God does not merely *tell* creation what is right. **God designed creation so that life functions properly only when it operates in harmony with what is right.** God's moral law is an expression of His own character, particularly His self-giving love. ([comeandreason.com](https://comeandreason.com/design-laws-of-god/))

That single idea becomes the framework through which the entire biblical story is understood.

## 1. GOD'S LAW FLOWS FROM WHO GOD IS

The foundation is not first, “God makes laws.”

The foundation is:

**God is love.**

Because God is love, everything He creates is designed to operate according to love. Love is not simply something God commands creatures to practice. Love originates in God's own being.

Biblical love is fundamentally **other-centered, giving, beneficent, self-sacrificial love** rather than self-seeking.

This is why Scripture says that love fulfills the law. God's moral law and God's character cannot ultimately be separated.

# 2. CREATOR LAW IS DIFFERENT FROM HUMAN LAW

Human governments create rules:

> Drive 55 mph.
> Pay this tax.
> Obtain this license.

Nothing inherent in nature causes a penalty when these rules are violated. An authority must detect the violation and impose a consequence.

the law of love and lifes work differently.

If someone jumps from a cliff, gravity does not need a judge to pronounce a sentence. If someone stops breathing, the law of respiration does not need an executioner. The consequence is intrinsic to leaving the conditions necessary for life.

The same principle is applied to God's moral law.

God's law is **the law upon which life is constructed to operate**. Therefore, violating God's law damages the person violating it.

The ultimate issue with sin is consequently not merely:

**“I broke God's rule.”**

It is:

**“I have departed from the way life was designed to function.”**

# 3. THE LAW OF LOVE

The most fundamental moral the law of love and life is love.

Love gives.

Selfishness takes.

Love seeks another person's good.

Selfishness uses another person for self.

Love protects individuality.

Selfishness controls.

Love sacrifices self for another.

Selfishness sacrifices others for self.

This is why Paul says love “does not seek its own” and why Christ's self-sacrificing life is the fullest revelation of divine love.

# 4. THE LAW OF LIBERTY

Love cannot be forced.

A person may be forced to behave in a certain way, but that person cannot be forced to love.

Therefore, freedom is built into God's government.

God presents truth, reveals love, appeals, warns, teaches, disciplines, persuades, and allows evidence to work upon the mind—but genuine loyalty must ultimately be freely given.

This is why Scripture repeatedly calls people to **choose**.

God's government operates through **truth, love, and liberty**, not coercion.

# 6. THE LAW OF WORSHIP: WE BECOME LIKE WHAT WE ADMIRE

If we behold the true God of self-giving love, truth, freedom, mercy, righteousness, and faithfulness, we become increasingly like Him.

# 7. WHAT SIN ACTUALLY IS

Under the law of love and life, sin is much larger than breaking a rule.

Sin is **departure from God's design for life**.

It begins internally.

Adam and Eve believed lies about God.

Believing the lie damaged trust.

Broken trust produced fear.

Fear turned attention toward protecting self.

Self-protection developed into selfishness.

Selfishness produced sinful behavior.

Therefore:

**lies → distrust → fear → selfishness → sinful acts → suffering → death**

This becomes one of the central diagnostic chains of the law of love and life theology.

# 21. THE WRITTEN LAW POINTS TO THE DEEPER LAW OF LOVE

The Ten Commandments accurately express God's moral will, but the written code was especially needed in a fallen world.

“Do not murder” is necessary because fallen people murder.

A person filled with divine love does not merely refrain from murder because a command prohibits it. The transformed person no longer desires murder because God's love operates within the heart.

Thus the written commandments diagnose and teach, while the eternal design principle beneath them is love.

# 23. GOD'S WRATH IS GOD “GIVING UP” OR “LETTING GO”

Romans 1 provides the controlling biblical pattern.

Paul announces the wrath of God and then repeatedly explains what God does:

**God gave them over.**

God's wrath is therefore understood as God finally respecting persistent rejection and ceasing to shield people from the consequences of the path they have chosen.

God does not abandon people casually.

He appeals, disciplines, warns, protects, restrains, heals, and pursues.

But love does not destroy freedom.

Eventually God allows a person determined to reject Him to experience what separation from God actually produces.

Love requires freedom.

↓

Satan rejected God's design and introduced lies about God's character.

↓

Humanity believed those lies.

↓

Lies destroyed trust.

↓

Distrust produced fear.

↓

Fear produced selfishness.

↓

Selfishness produced sinful actions.

↓

Sin damaged humanity and separated humanity from the Source of life.

↓

↓

Human beings inherited this damaged condition and could not repair humanity themselves.

↓

Jesus became truly human—the second Adam.

↓

He revealed the truth about God and destroyed Satan's lies.

↓

Within humanity He overcame every temptation toward fear-driven selfishness.

↓

He developed a perfectly loving, trusting, righteous human character.

↓

↓

He defeated Satan, sin, selfishness, and death in humanity.

↓

He rose with a perfected, victorious human life.

↓

Through faith—trust—we become united with Christ.

↓

The Holy Spirit takes what Christ accomplished and reproduces His life within us.

↓

Lies are replaced with truth.

↓

Distrust is replaced with trust.

↓

Fear is replaced with love.

↓

Selfishness is replaced with Christlike love.

↓

God writes His law into our hearts and minds.

↓

We become new creations.

↓

Obedience becomes the fruit of restoration rather than frightened rule-keeping.

↓

Judgment ultimately reveals what every person has freely chosen to become.

↓

Those restored to harmony with God live eternally because they are connected with the Source of life.

↓

Those who permanently reject God ultimately experience God's wrath—God allowing them the separation they have persistently chosen.

↓

Unremedied sin produces its inevitable result: the second death.

↓

Sin, Satan, suffering, and death disappear forever.

↓

The universe returns to perfect harmony with the Creator and His eternal the law of love and life.
