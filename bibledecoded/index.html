YOUR PATH THROUGH THE COURSE

Six lessons.
A lasting set of skills.

Start with a solid foundation, then practice a different way of exploring Scripture in each lesson.

01

Foundations

Key ideas and interpretive guardrails that help you approach the Bible with greater clarity.

02

Look for Christ

Learn to ask, “Where is Jesus in this passage?” and see Christ-centered connections throughout Scripture.

03

Pattern Recognition

Break passages into steps, find repeated sequences elsewhere in Scripture, and ask what the repeated pattern teaches.

04

The Questioning Method

Use Who? What? When? Where? Why? and How? to turn curiosity into deeper Bible investigation.

05

Exegesis

Learn to draw meaning out of the text instead of reading your own assumptions into it.

06

Bible Memorization

Hide Scripture in your mind through repetition, emphasis, chapters, topic maps, and Scripture songs.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Make yourself at home.

Do I need to be experienced at Bible study?

No. Begin with Foundations and move at your own pace. The exercises give you a place to practice each method.

Do I need another password?

You can continue with Google or ask for an email sign-in link. Use the same email address you used at checkout.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. The course works on phones, tablets, and computers. Your saved answers belong to your account, so you can return on another device.

Can I print the workbooks?

Yes. Each lesson includes a printable workbook. You can also print your completed online answers.

What happens when I finish?

Your Study Lab unlocks after the six main lessons. Keep creating and revisiting personal Bible studies using the course methods.
