TRY JESUS: THE JOURNEY

Delete your app account and data.

This page provides the official account-deletion path for Try Jesus: The Journey, published by Try Jesus Media.

OPTION 1 · IN THE APP

Delete immediately while signed in

  1. Open Try Jesus: The Journey.
  2. Choose More, then find the Account section.
  3. Select Delete My Account and Data.
  4. Review the warning and confirm the permanent deletion.

The in-app process deletes the account and associated app data after the final confirmation.

OPTION 2 · WITHOUT THE APP

Send an account-deletion request

Email us from the address associated with your Try Jesus account. Use the subject “Try Jesus Journey account deletion request” and state that you want the account and associated data deleted.

Email an account-deletion request

info@tryjesusmedia.com

We may ask you to verify account ownership. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

DELETE SELECTED DATA

Keep your account and remove only what you choose

You can delete an individual Prayer Journal entry from the Prayer Journal by selecting Delete on that entry and confirming. You can also undo a completed reading from its reading-plan screen.

For help deleting other selected synced data without deleting your account, email us from the address associated with your Try Jesus account. Use the subject “Try Jesus Journey data deletion request” and name the data you want removed.

Email a selected-data deletion request

info@tryjesusmedia.com

We may ask you to verify account ownership. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

WHAT IS DELETED

Account and associated app data

Deletion removes the authentication account and app data associated with it, including profile information, synced reading progress, Prayer Journal entries, submitted questions, saved Pastor Kal chat history, Conflict of the Ages progress, principles, cross-references, and discussion activity.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Limited request correspondence or records that must be retained for security or legal obligations may be kept as described in our Privacy Policy.
