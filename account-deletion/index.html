Delete your app account and data.

This page provides the official account-deletion paths for Try Jesus: The Journey and Bible and Conflict of the Ages, developed by Try Jesus Media.

OPTION 1 · IN THE APP

Delete immediately while signed in

Try Jesus: The Journey

  1. Open the app and follow More → Account → Delete My Account and Data.
  2. Review the warning and confirm the permanent deletion.

Bible and Conflict of the Ages

  1. Open the app and follow Menu → Account & privacy → Delete My Account and Data.
  2. Review the warning and confirm the permanent deletion.

Both apps use the same shared Account. The in-app process deletes that Account and associated synced data for both apps and their corresponding website reading plans, including all shared synced progress, after the final confirmation. In Bible and Conflict of the Ages, it also removes all reading progress, Journey Points, and other app data stored on that device.

OPTION 2 · WITHOUT THE APP

Send an account-deletion request

Email us from the address associated with your shared Try Jesus Media account. Use the subject “Try Jesus Media apps account deletion request” and state that you want the Account and associated data for both apps deleted, including all shared synced progress.

Email an account-deletion request

info@tryjesusmedia.com

We may ask you to verify account ownership. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

WHAT IS DELETED

Account and associated app data

Deletion removes the shared authentication account and app data associated with it across both Try Jesus: The Journey and Bible and Conflict of the Ages, including profile information, all shared synced reading progress, Journey Points, Prayer Journal entries, submitted questions, saved Pastor Kal chat history, Conflict of the Ages progress, principles, cross-references, and discussion activity.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Limited request correspondence or records that must be retained for security or legal obligations may be kept as described in our Privacy Policy.
