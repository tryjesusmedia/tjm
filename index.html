No. The guides and discussions are approachable for beginners while still meaningful for experienced Bible readers.

No. You may use the guides completely on your own. The live discussion is an optional second gift.

No. You are welcome to listen privately with your camera and microphone off.

Absolutely. Individuals, couples, families, and friends are welcome.

Why do you request my mobile number?

It may be used to send requested access links, helpful reminders, and occasional Bible discussion invitations. Your information is never sold.

No. This is a respectful opportunity to explore Scripture and decide what you believe for yourself.
